Who We Are

Specialized U.S. Apparel DDP/LDP Freight Forwarding

1. We provide end-to-end DDP/LDP logistics solutions specifically for apparel shipments to the United States. We focus exclusively on the U.S. market and apparel cargo, delivering safer and more specialized services.

2. Our founder has a background in U.S. customs brokerage. We have equity interests in and partnerships with 15–20 customs brokerage firms, some of which are CTPAT-certified. We use U.S.-based Importers of Record (IORs).

Our Services

Our Core Focus

We specialize in DDP freight forwarding for apparel shipments to the United States. We understand the challenges of apparel exports, including high tariffs, complex product classifications, and customs inspections. Rather than offering a broad range of generic services, we focus on specialized expertise, providing traditional apparel exporters with reliable door-to-door logistics solutions.

When it comes to apparel DDP shipping, choosing a specialist who focuses exclusively on apparel is better than choosing a forwarder that handles everything. Let us manage the complexities of tariffs and customs clearance, so you can give your U.S. buyers reliable delivery and peace of mind.

A Detailed Overview of Our DDP Logistics Services for Apparel Customers

Exporters:Tight production schedules and urgent delivery deadlines are common. Apparel factories often work overtime to complete orders, which can lead to late shipments, price discounts, and chargebacks requested by overseas buyers.

Importers:Apparel is a seasonal product, and overseas buyers have strict requirements regarding bulk shipment delivery schedules.


Based on these characteristics of the apparel industry, we have developed customized, end-to-end logistics solutions specifically for apparel customers. Our primary objectives are to ensure: 1. Cargo Safety 2. Delivery Timeliness. Every detail of our customized services is designed around these two priorities, helping apparel customers protect their shipments while meeting demanding delivery schedules.

China Domestic Trucking Fleet
CENTEN provides door-to-door services, with domestic pickup and delivery handled by our professional trucking fleet.

Our trucking partners have extensive experience handling the loading schedules and time-sensitive requirements of apparel shipments. They understand that garment factories frequently work overtime to meet production deadlines, so they can provide 24-hour standby operations when necessary. We carefully select our trucking partners and prioritize newer vehicles to help ensure reliable transit times on highways. Our operations team also maintains close, seamless control over the entire trucking process, providing greater service reliability.

China Customs Brokerage
CENTEN works exclusively with one dedicated customs brokerage firm for all export customs clearance operations in China.

Our customs brokerage partner has worked closely with us for many years and has extensive expertise in HS classification for apparel products and brand-related customs requirements. The team is also highly experienced in handling customs inspections at ports of origin, particularly for apparel shipments.

Ocean Carriers
We are a Tier-1 authorized agent for multiple ocean carriers in China, including HEDE and COSCO, ensuring reliable space allocation and service support.

Our strong carrier relationships help minimize the risk of space shortages during peak seasons and reduce the possibility of rolled containers. Our partnership with HEDE is particularly important for our customized apparel logistics services. As HEDE’s largest Tier-1 agent, we have significant influence over space allocation.

Port Gate-In
Understanding the tight delivery schedules and late gate-in requirements of apparel customers, we maintain close cooperation with port terminals and ocean carriers to accommodate late gate-in arrangements.

For HEDE and Matson shipments in particular, we can arrange container gate-in as late as one day before vessel departure, helping ensure that containers are loaded as planned without being rolled. Under special circumstances, we can even arrange same-day gate-in and vessel loading when the vessel departs that evening. This gives apparel customers more time for production and transportation.

Overseas Customer Service
We have a dedicated overseas customer service team that coordinates seamlessly with destination agents across different time zones.

This ensures that instructions from our customers in China are communicated to overseas teams promptly, minimizing operational errors caused by time differences. All shipment-related operational instructions from China are consolidated by our overseas customer service team, which then communicates directly and accurately with destination agents.

Destination Agents & Customs Clearance
Our destination-side customs brokerage network includes our own operations and 15–20 partner customs brokerage firms.

1. We diversify customs clearance risks to maximize the protection of both our customers and their shipments. 2. Each customs brokerage partner has specific responsibilities based on different product categories and shipment characteristics. For example, we assign different brokers to lightweight cargo such as padded jackets and outerwear, heavy cargo such as woven workwear, low-value goods, and high-value goods. This allows us to provide highly specialized customs clearance for different types of apparel. Since apparel shipments are subject to strict delivery deadlines, there is little room for delays caused by customs inspections. We therefore work to maximize the likelihood of smooth customs clearance. 3. Our destination agents are familiar with the warehouse appointment requirements of most major apparel importers in the United States. They can independently arrange delivery appointments with overseas warehouses without requiring instructions from customers in China.

U.S. Domestic Trucking Fleet
Our U.S. trucking partners operate on standby to ensure that apparel shipments are delivered to customers’ warehouses on time.

Based on vessel arrival schedules, our destination agents issue dispatch instructions to trucking partners, requiring drivers to arrive at the terminal at least 3–6 hours in advance and remain on standby. We primarily use Chinese-speaking drivers to ensure reliable communication, professional work attitudes, and operational efficiency. We do not use Mexican or local U.S. drivers. Our drivers are highly familiar with the container yard layouts and container locations of major carriers, including HEDE, Matson, and COSCO, allowing them to locate and retrieve the correct containers as quickly as possible.

Customs Bonds (BOND)
We maintain an annual inventory of 300–500 customs bonds to meet apparel customers’ security requirements, with one dedicated bond allocated to each container.

One Bond per Container is a policy that many freight forwarders advertise. However, our advantage goes beyond this arrangement: we maintain a highly specialized and consistent customs bond profile. We focus exclusively on apparel shipments, and from the perspective of U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP), our bonds are associated with legitimate U.S.-based importers engaged in regular apparel imports. In contrast, many other DDP freight forwarders handle a wide range of products, including auto parts, home furnishings, footwear, hats, and bags. This makes it difficult to maintain a consistent bond history. Although they may also provide one bond per container, their bond records can include a mixture of furniture, auto parts, apparel, footwear, and accessories. Such diversified import records may attract greater scrutiny from U.S. Customs. This also helps explain why many e-commerce freight forwarders experienced significant difficulties during the 5H inspections that began in February 2026, while our traditional DDP operations had no containers subject to 5H inspections.

Dual Customs Specialist Review
Both our China and overseas offices have dedicated customs specialists.

After discussions and coordination between our sales team and the customer, we prepare the corresponding quotation. Before issuing a quotation, our internal procedures require the sales team to submit the relevant customer and product information to our China-based customs specialist. The China team then works with our overseas customs specialist to review and verify the details. The sales team can only provide the final quotation after both customs specialists have approved it. This dual-review mechanism establishes a compliance and risk-control barrier before the shipment is dispatched, helping maximize the safety of apparel shipments. Apparel shipments operate under tight delivery schedules and cannot afford the significant delays that customs inspections may cause.
